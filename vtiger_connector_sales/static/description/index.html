Odoo - vtiger Connector Sales

The vtiger-Odoo Sales Sync module is designed to help businesses seamlessly integrate and synchronize sales orders, customer details, product pricing, and payment information between Odoo and vtiger CRM. This automation eliminates manual data entry, reduces errors, and ensures real-time updates for a smoother sales workflow.

Highlighted-features

Effortless Data Management

Eliminates manual data entry errors, ensuring all sales transactions are recorded correctly. Supports bulk sales order imports, streamlining large-scale sales operations.

Real-Time Sales Order Sync

Automatically synchronizes sales orders, quotations, and modifications between Odoo and vtiger CRM in real time. Ensures instant updates whenever a sale is confirmed, modified, or canceled, preventing data discrepancies.

Conflict Resolution Mechanism

Detects and resolves discrepancies in sales orders, customer details, and product pricing between Odoo and vtiger CRM. Maintains a detailed audit log for tracking and compliance purposes.

Real-Time Sync Status

Displays live synchronization updates, ensuring customer orders, payments, and invoices are always up to date. Provides instant alerts for sync failures, duplicate data, or missing order details.

User-Friendly Interface

Designed with an intuitive dashboard, offering quick access to all sales data. Provides one-click synchronization, making it easy to update orders, invoices, and customer details.

Sales Details Sync

Automatically synchronizes sales orders, quotations, and order modifications between Odoo and vtiger CRM. Ensures real-time updates whenever a sale is confirmed, canceled, or modified.

  • Navigate to the Settings section, and then select the Companies option.
  • Under Vtiger Configuration, enter the required details, including the access key, Vtiger server URL, and username.

  • Navigate to the Inventory page, as shown in the image above and then move to Sales Order section.

  • You can create a new Sales by clicking on the "Add Sales Order" button.

  • At this stage, you must input all the relevant Sales Order details to successfully create the Sales.
  • Save the Sales Order information.

  • You will now observe that the Sales Order has been successfully created and saved.

  • Navigate to the Vtiger Configuration section, where you will find options to synchronize data.
  • Choose the Sync Sales Order Button to sync the data of the Sales Order.

  • You will now observe that the Sales Order and its details have been successfully imported.

  • Here you will see the details of the Sales Order.

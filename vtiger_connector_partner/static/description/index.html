Odoo - vtiger Connector Partner

This module enables businesses to seamlessly synchronize customer contacts, company details, addresses, and communication history between Odoo and vtiger. This automation eliminates duplicate entries, prevents data inconsistencies, and ensures real-time updates across both systems.

Highlighted-features

Effortless Data Management

Eliminates manual data entry errors, ensuring contact records are always accurate. Enables bulk imports of the contact data, saving time in large-scale operations.

Real-Time Contact Sync

Instantly syncs contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, company details, and job titles. Ensures that any modifications made in vtiger or Odoo are reflected in real-time across both platforms.

Conflict Resolution Mechanism

Detects and resolves contact data mismatches between Odoo and vtiger CRM. Keeps an audit trail of changes, ensuring compliance and data integrity.

Contact Data Sync

Automatically syncs contact names, phone numbers, email addresses, company details, and job titles between Odoo and vtiger CRM. Ensures real-time updates for contact additions, modifications, and deletions, keeping records consistent across platforms.

User-Friendly Interface

Features an intuitive dashboard, offering quick access to contact details. Provides one-click synchronization, making it effortless to update and manage contacts.

Real-Time Sync Status

Ensures instant updates when a new contact is added, modified, or deleted in either system. Displays live synchronization status, allowing users to track updates in real-time.

  • Navigate to the Settings section, and then select the Companies option.
  • Under Vtiger Configuration, enter the required details, including the access key, Vtiger server URL, and username.

  • Next, navigate to the Contacts page, as shown in the image above.

  • You can create a new contact by clicking on the "Add Contact" button.

  • At this stage, you must input all the relevant contact details to successfully create the contact.

  • Save the contact information.

  • You will now observe that the contact has been successfully created and saved.

  • Navigate to the Vtiger Configuration section, where you will find options to synchronize data.
  • Choose the Sync Partner Button to sync the data of the Contact.

  • You will now observe that the contact and its details have been successfully imported.
